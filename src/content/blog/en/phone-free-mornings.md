Every morning, the first thing I did was scroll. This week I tried the opposite.

## Days 1–2: restless

My hand kept reaching for the phone. I had to leave it in another room.

## Days 3–5: quieter

I made coffee, looked out the window, wrote three lines in my notebook. <mark>The morning felt strangely longer.</mark>

## Days 6–7: no going back

I don't think I'll do it forever, but I'll definitely keep it more often.

> A slow morning makes the whole day less rushed.
