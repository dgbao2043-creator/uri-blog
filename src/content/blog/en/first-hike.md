5am, the group set off. I was the slowest.

## Halfway

At one point I wanted to turn back. Someone in the group said: "Don't look at the top, just the next ten steps." <mark>Ten steps, then ten more.</mark>

## At the top

The sun turned the whole valley gold. I didn't get a single good photo — I was too busy looking.

## Takeaways

- Going slow is fine; stopping is what to worry about.
- Bring more water than you think.
- The best photo is sometimes the one you don't take.
