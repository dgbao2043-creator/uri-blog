Last month I decided to teach myself Japanese. No classes, no teacher — just me, two notebooks and a flashcard app.

## Week one: way too eager

I studied three hours a day. By day five I… stopped opening the book. Lesson one: <mark>motivation runs out fast</mark>, habits don't.

## What I changed

- Only **20 minutes every morning**, before touching my phone.
- Hand-write every new word in a notebook, even with the app.
- Review on weekends and highlight the words I still forget.

## After 30 days

Nothing huge: the alphabets, about 200 words, a few simple sentences. But for the first time I studied something for a whole month without quitting.

---

If you're starting something new too, try starting really small.
