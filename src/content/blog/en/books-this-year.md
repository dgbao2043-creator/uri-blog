I read 14 books this year. These are the 5 I still think about.

1. **Atomic Habits** — it got me to start with the smallest thing.
2. **Deep Work** — I realised my phone takes more than I thought.
3. **Norwegian Wood** — sometimes you need a book just to be sad with.
4. **Show Your Work!** — the reason this blog exists.
5. **The Art of Focus** — surprisingly practical.

What I learned: <mark>reading slowly and taking notes</mark> helps me remember far more than rushing to finish.
